What this site is

À l'affût is a free tool that shows the job openings published by the companies you choose to follow. It is independent and not affiliated with those companies, nor with the job boards they use (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, or any RSS or Atom feed). Job titles, locations, dates and links belong to their publishers and are shown as they provide them. Always check an opening on the company's own page before applying.

Removal requests

If you represent a company and do not want its openings to be followable on this site:

  1. Write to [CONTACT EMAIL] from an address of the company's domain.
  2. Include the link to your careers page or to one of your job openings.

Once the request is confirmed, the source is blocked: it can no longer be added, visitors' browsers stop requesting it, and its openings are removed from the lists of visitors who followed it the next time they open the site.
