Data & privacy

Nothing is stored on our servers

There is no account and no sign-up. Our server only delivers the pages of this site; it never receives the companies you follow, the jobs you see or the marks you set.

What stays in your browser

The companies you follow, the openings found, and your marks (“Applied”, “Not interested”) are kept in your browser's local storage, on your device only. Clearing this site's data in your browser erases them. Use Export in Companies to keep a copy as a file, and Import to restore it, for example on another device.

Requests made by your browser

To find openings, your browser contacts the public job board of each company you follow, directly:

  • Greenhouse: boards-api.greenhouse.io
  • Lever: api.lever.co and api.eu.lever.co
  • Ashby: api.ashbyhq.com
  • SmartRecruiters: api.smartrecruiters.com
  • RSS or Atom feeds: the address you added

Like any website you visit, these services see your IP address and the page requested. Their own privacy policies apply. This site does not pass them anything else about you.

No tracking

No cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party scripts.
